# robots.txt: appear in AI search answers, opt out of AI model training
# Template from https://github.com/Cited-io/ai-search-crawlers-robots-txt
# Tokens verified against each vendor's own documentation on 2026-10-15.
# Full table with purposes and IP lists:
#   https://github.com/Cited-io/ai-search-crawlers-robots-txt/blob/main/crawlers.md
#
# What this file does:
#   ALLOWS  the search crawlers that show and link your pages in
#           ChatGPT search, Claude, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and
#           AI Mode, Copilot, Siri, Meta AI, Alexa, DuckDuckGo and Mistral.
#   ALLOWS  the fetchers that load a page when a person asks about it.
#   BLOCKS  the crawlers and tokens that control use for model training.
#
# HOW TO EDIT
# 1. Replace https://www.example.com with your domain (bottom of file).
# 2. Put any private paths (admin, cart, account, search results) under
#    EVERY group, not just under "User-agent: *".
#    Under RFC 9309 a crawler follows only the most specific group that
#    names it. A crawler named in group 2 ignores the rules in group 1.
# 3. Read the two trade-offs below before you publish.
# 4. Check what is actually served after your CDN with check.md:
#    https://github.com/Cited-io/ai-search-crawlers-robots-txt/blob/main/check.md
#
# TRADE-OFF 1: Google-Extended is not training-only.
#   Google says it also controls grounding in the Gemini app and in
#   Grounding with Google Search on Vertex AI. Blocking it can keep your
#   pages out of Gemini app answers. It does NOT affect Google Search,
#   AI Overviews or AI Mode, which follow Googlebot.
#   Want Gemini app answers too? Move Google-Extended to group 2.
#   https://developers.google.com/crawling/docs/crawlers-fetchers/google-common-crawlers
#
# TRADE-OFF 2: Bing and Copilot have no training token.
#   Microsoft documents no separate Copilot crawler and no robots.txt
#   training opt-out. Its controls are page-level meta tags:
#     NOCACHE   -> may appear in AI answers as URL, title and snippet only;
#                  only those may be used for training.
#     NOARCHIVE -> left out of AI answers and not used for training.
#   Keep bingbot allowed if you want Copilot citations.
#   https://blogs.bing.com/webmaster/september-2023/Announcing-new-options-for-webmasters-to-control-usage-of-their-content-in-Bing-Chat
#
# robots.txt is a request, not a lock. Some user fetchers say they may
# not follow it (ChatGPT-User, Perplexity-User, meta-externalfetcher,
# Amzn-User). To enforce a block you need a firewall rule; see:
#   https://github.com/Cited-io/ai-search-crawlers-robots-txt/blob/main/templates/cloudflare-vercel.md
