# don't mind your public pages being used to train models.
#
# HOW TO EDIT
# 1. Replace https://www.example.com with your domain (bottom of file).
# 2. Put any private paths (admin, cart, account, search results) under
#    EVERY group below, not just under "User-agent: *".
#    Under RFC 9309 a crawler follows only the most specific group that
#    names it. A crawler named in group 2 ignores the rules in group 1.
# 3. Serve the file at the root: https://www.example.com/robots.txt
# 4. Check what is actually served after your CDN with check.md:
#    https://github.com/Cited-io/ai-search-crawlers-robots-txt/blob/main/check.md
#
# robots.txt is a request, not a lock. It cannot override a firewall
# or bot rule that blocks the crawler before it reads this file. See:
#   https://github.com/Cited-io/ai-search-crawlers-robots-txt/blob/main/templates/cloudflare-vercel.md
