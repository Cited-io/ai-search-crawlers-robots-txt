# Company Name

> One or two sentences: what you are, what you sell, and who it is for. Use the words your buyers use, not a slogan. Example: "Company Name is payroll software for US restaurants with 5 to 200 staff, with tip pooling and scheduling built in."

Key facts:

- Category: the plain name of what you sell, as a buyer would search for it.
- Customers: who it is for (industry, size, region).
- Locations or markets served: countries, regions or cities.
- Pricing: starting price and billing period, with the month you checked it.
- Getting started: free trial, demo, or contact sales.
- Contact: a monitored email address.
