# Example Payroll

> Example Payroll is payroll and scheduling software for independent restaurants in the United States with 5 to 200 staff. It runs payroll, pools and distributes tips, files federal and state payroll taxes, and builds staff schedules from forecast covers.

Example Payroll is a fictional company used to show the llms.txt format. Every fact below is invented.

Key facts:

- Category: restaurant payroll software, with tip pooling and staff scheduling.
- Customers: independent restaurants, cafes and bars with 5 to 200 staff.
- Markets served: all 50 US states. Not available outside the United States.
- Pricing (checked October 2026): from $6 per employee per month plus a $49 monthly base fee. No setup fee.
- Getting started: a 30-day free trial, or book a demo.
- Integrations: imports hours and tips from the major restaurant point-of-sale systems.
- Contact: sales@example.com

## Product

- [Payroll](https://www.example.com/payroll): unlimited pay runs, direct deposit and payroll tax filing in every state.
